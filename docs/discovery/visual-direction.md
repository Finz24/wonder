# Wonder visual direction

The refined prototype was accepted as the direction for the next phase on 2026-09-07. The implementation brief is [Wonder v1](../specs/wonder-v1.md); the prototype remains the visual reference, with production motion quality and behavior still to be implemented.

## Accepted direction

- A tactile studio journal presented as a dimensional scroll, with substantial animation and realistic material behavior. The Ruth reference is exclusively about the physical scroll mechanism.
- Stylized realism: wooden rollers, parchment fibers, curls, tension, shadows, and changing light against an abstract warm studio atmosphere.
- A closed cover displaying Wonder, By Hila, and the application symbol. The Visitor's first scroll movement initiates the opening.
- Native vertical scrolling reveals Project photographs and descriptions. Photography appears placed on the parchment with subtle depth and editorial variation.
- The scroll mechanics carry the strongest motion; secondary animation supports the work's readability. Sound is off by default.
- Every Published Project is discoverable within the scroll. There is no public search bar or separate index. Project links land inside the scroll.
- The ending closes the scroll around accessible contact methods. WhatsApp remains available throughout the journey.
- Reduced-motion and static presentations preserve access to the same content.
- Testimonials appear within their associated Project chapters; selected excerpts may also appear near the closing section.
- Hila's Owner Workspace uses a calm, conventional editing interface with media ordering and publication controls. Preview shows the public scroll experience.

## Prototype refinement requested

- The calm Owner Workspace shares the public scroll's shapes, palette, and typography, including parchment-like panels and wax-red actions.
- Visitors can read and expand Project-bound Testimonials within the scroll; Hila's Project preview includes published Testimonials.

- Keep main Project photography compact so it shares the chapter with its description. Provide selectable previews of multiple images within the scroll.
- Refine the seal toward realistic wax with varied pastel-red tones and a flowing handwritten W intertwined with H and O.
- Include the Owner Workspace in design exploration now, using temporary local interactions; authentication, durable storage, and production behavior remain implementation work.

## Wax seal symbol

The application symbol takes the form of a wax seal with a minimal design and a soft, handmade material feeling reminiscent of plastilina. The owner clarified that "wine mark" meant "wax seal" and supplied a reference image showing irregular wax rims, impressed emblems, and dimensional surfaces in several colors.

The supplied image guides the seal's physical form and texture. The approved emblem is a custom W forming the outer structure, with H nestled inside its left wing and O inside its right wing, referring to Hila Orfali. The letters are impressed into the wax as one monogram: W reads first, while H and O reward closer inspection. Keep the rim slightly irregular and the surface softly matte. Warm burgundy is the proposed starting color for visual exploration, rather than a finalized brand color.
