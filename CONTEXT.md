# Wonder

Wonder presents Hila's celebration work and turns a Visitor's interest into a direct conversation with her about personalized Products for their own Occasion.

## Language

**Creator**:
Hila, Wonder's owner and sole Creator. She personally makes the celebration objects and decorations presented by Wonder and independently curates and publishes the Portfolio.
_Avoid_: Seller, vendor, event planner

**Visitor**:
A private individual exploring the Creator's work while imagining something for their own Occasion.
_Avoid_: Shopper, buyer, applicant

**Client**:
The person who arranges work with the Creator. A Client is distinct from a recipient or the person being celebrated when those are different people.
_Avoid_: Account, anonymous subject of a Project Description

**Occasion**:
A celebration for which a Visitor may want one or more Products.
_Avoid_: Event-planning engagement

**Product**:
An individual physical object or decorative piece personally made by the Creator and shown within a Project.
_Avoid_: Third-party good, event-planning service, separately purchasable catalogue item

**Project**:
A public Portfolio entry with a title, description, selected cover, and either one photo or an ordered album. It may show one Product, Products from one Occasion, or Products connected by a shared theme without exposing a formal Project type to Visitors.
_Avoid_: Event-planning engagement, mandatory synonym for one client commission

**Project Description**:
A first-person account in Hila's feminine voice accompanying a Project. It explains the work without identifying a Client or showing Client faces.
_Avoid_: Client profile, case study containing personal details

**Creator Study**:
A clearly labelled, self-initiated Project through which the Creator explores or demonstrates an idea that was not made for a Client.
_Avoid_: Fictional Client Project

**Portfolio**:
The Creator's curated public collection of Projects, intended to advertise her work and inspire relevant Inquiries.
_Avoid_: Store, marketplace, product catalogue

**Service**:
Hila's work of creating one or more personalized Products for a Visitor's Occasion.
_Avoid_: Event planning, product resale, fixed catalogue purchase

**Inquiry**:
A first contact initiated when a Visitor follows a Portfolio link to send Hila a WhatsApp message about creating Products for their Occasion. A Project may provide the inspiration and context for that message.
_Avoid_: Order, on-site inquiry form, in-product chat

**Testimonial**:
A Client's account of their experience, submitted through a private Project-bound link sent by Hila and attached to exactly one Project. Its text is immutable; Hila may publish it, unpublish it, or delete it.
_Avoid_: Creator-edited endorsement, unattached quote, required Project content

**Owner Workspace**:
Hila's private area within Wonder for creating, editing, previewing, publishing, and archiving Projects and for reviewing and managing Testimonials. Hila is its only user.
_Avoid_: Multi-creator dashboard, Client account area, public administration page

**Testimonial Invitation**:
A private link Hila sends to a Client for one predetermined Project. It accepts one Testimonial submission and then closes, without granting access to the Owner Workspace.
_Avoid_: Public testimonial form, Project selector

## Publication

**Draft**:
Project content being prepared privately by the Creator and not visible in the public Portfolio.

**Preview**:
A private presentation of Project changes as they would appear if published.

**Published**:
The Project version currently visible to Visitors in the public Portfolio.

**Archived**:
A Project removed from the public Portfolio but retained by the Creator.
